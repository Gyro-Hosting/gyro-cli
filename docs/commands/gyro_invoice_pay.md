## gyro invoice pay

Pay an invoice from the account balance

### Synopsis

Pay an invoice from the account balance

Balance only (never a card). Requires `"confirm": true` and an `Idempotency-Key` header.

API: POST /cloud/invoices/{id}/pay (operationId payInvoiceFromBalance, scope billing_pay)

```
gyro invoice pay <ID> [flags]
```
