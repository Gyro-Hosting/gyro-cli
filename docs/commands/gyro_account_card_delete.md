## gyro account card delete

Delete a saved card (also at the bank)

### Synopsis

Delete a saved card (also at the bank)

`"confirm": true` required. The card is removed from the account and deleted at the bank; `bank` = removed | failed (retried automatically).

API: DELETE /cloud/account/cards/{id} (operationId deleteSavedCard, scope billing_pay)

```
gyro account card delete <ID> [flags]
```
