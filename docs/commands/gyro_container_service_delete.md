## gyro container service delete

Delete a container service

### Synopsis

Delete a container service

Deletes the service, its address, secrets, revision history and certificates. Needs `"confirm": true`.

API: DELETE /cloud/containers/{id}/services/{name} (operationId typed_delete_containers__id__services__name, scope services_write)

```
gyro container service delete <ID> <NAME> [flags]
```
